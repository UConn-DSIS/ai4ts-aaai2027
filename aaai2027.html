AAAI'27 Workshop




AI4TS: AI for Time Series


Analysis:




Theory, Algorithms, and Applications


Time series data are becoming ubiquitous across numerous real-world applications, including IoT devices, healthcare, wearable devices, smart vehicles, financial markets, biological sciences, and environmental sciences. The availability of massive amounts of data, their complex underlying structures and distributions, and advances in high-performance computing have created a growing demand for new theories and algorithms to address fundamental challenges in time series analysis, including representation, classification, prediction, causal analysis, and multimodal learning across diverse applications.

The goal of this workshop is to provide a platform for researchers and AI practitioners from academia and industry to discuss emerging research directions, key technical challenges, and solutions for addressing these challenges in practical applications. The workshop will focus on both the theoretical and practical aspects of time series data analysis and aims to inspire innovations in theories, algorithms, and applications, especially in emerging domains such as multimodal learning and agentic AI. We will invite researchers, AI practitioners, and policymakers from related fields, including machine learning, data science, statistics, econometrics, and other disciplines, to contribute to the workshop.

This workshop welcomes submissions presenting innovative solutions to a broad range of time series analysis problems. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Time series forecasting and prediction
  • Spatio-temporal forecasting and prediction
  • Time series anomaly detection and diagnosis
  • Time series change point detection
  • Time series classification and clustering
  • Time series similarity search
  • Time series indexing
  • Time series compression
  • Time series pattern discovery
  • Time series domain adaptation and generalization
  • Time series source-free domain adaptation
  • Federated learning for time series
  • Interpretation and explanation in time series
  • Causal discovery and inference in time series
  • Bias and fairness in time series
  • Benchmarks, experimental evaluation, and comparison for time series analysis tasks
  • Foundation models and LLMs for time series analysis
  • Multimodal time series analysis
  • Agentic AI for time series analysis
  • Time series applications in various areas: E-commerce, Cloud computing, Transportation, Fintech, Healthcare, Internet of things, Wireless networks, Predictive maintenance, Education, Energy, Climate, etc.

Call for Papers

Contact with us: ai4ts.ijcai@gmail.com

Submissions should be 4-7 pages long, excluding references, and follow the AAAI2027 template. Submissions are double-blind and author identity should not be revealed to the reviewers. An optional appendix of arbitrary length is allowed and should be put at the end of the paper (after references).

Accepted papers will be presented as posters during the workshop and listed on the website (non-archival/without proceedings). A small number of accepted papers will be selected to be presented as contributed talks (10-minute oral presentations). We also welcome submissions of unpublished papers, including those submitted/accepted to other venues if that other venue allows.
